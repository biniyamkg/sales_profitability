===============================
Sales Profitability Report
===============================

This module provides a comprehensive Sales Profitability Reporting tool for Odoo 17 Community edition.
It allows you to analyze sales performance, calculate margins, and export clean, professional reports in both PDF and Excel formats.

Key Features
============

- Profitability Report from Sales Orders
- Accessible via **Sales > Reporting > Profitability Report**
- Filter by Date Range and Sales Teams
- Includes:
  - Product Name and Category
  - Quantity Sold
  - Unit Price and Unit Cost
  - Total Cost and Subtotal
  - Gross Margin and Margin Percentage
- Key KPIs:
  - Total Sales
  - Total Cost
  - Total Margin
  - Overall Margin Percentage
- Excel Report:
  - Well-formatted with column widths, styles, number formatting
  - KPI Summary at the top
- PDF Report:
  - Print-ready with company header and clean layout

Installation
============

To install this module:

1. Download the module and add it to your Odoo `addons` folder.
2. Restart your Odoo server.
3. Enable developer mode.
4. Go to the **Apps** menu and click on **Update Apps List**.
5. Search for "Sales Profitability Report" and click **Install**.

Upgrade
============

To upgrade this module:

1. Replace the old module code with the new version in your `addons` folder.
2. Restart your Odoo server.
3. Go to **Apps** > Search for "Sales Profitability Report" > Click **Upgrade**.

Configuration
=============

No additional configuration is required after installation.
To generate a report:

1. Go to **Sales > Reporting > Profitability Report**.
2. Select filters like Sales Team or Date Range.
3. Click **Print PDF** or **Export Excel**.

Frequently Asked Questions
==========================

**1. Does this module work on Odoo Community edition?**
Yes. Fully compatible with Odoo 17 Community edition.

**2. Can we export profitability reports to Excel?**
Yes. Excel exports are supported with professional formatting and summaries.

**3. Are costs calculated from product cost price?**
Yes. Unit cost is pulled from the product’s standard cost.

**4. Can we see margin percentages per product?**
Yes. Each line shows margin and margin percentage.
